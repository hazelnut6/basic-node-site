About

The Witches' Road is a fictional mystical pathway from the "Ballad of the Witches' Road" conceived by Nicholas Scratch and Agatha Harkness that promised to grant a traveler their deepest desire if they could endure and overcome its arcane trials. In 2026, the Road was unknowingly willed into existence by Billy Maximoff through chaos magic as a pocket dimension underneath Westview, New Jersey(MCU wiki, Witches' Road).
